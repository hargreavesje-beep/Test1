  • GoodAll three methods put you in Excellent for men aged 30 to 39 (48.3 to 54), and Garmin's 52 sits near the top of it. In large cohort studies, the top fitness bands are linked with the lowest long-term health risk.
  • Why they differThe power estimate assumes typical pedalling efficiency and is the most conservative; the heart-rate ratio tends to over-read. The truth is most likely 50 to 52. Use the power estimate for the trend, because it moves with your training data.
  • LeverSuperior (54+) is reachable at about 385 W for 5 minutes at 88 kg, or 370 W at 85 kg. You have held 385 W before. The 5 and 6 × 3 min at 350 W sessions in Block 1 are the direct VO2 work; weight is the second lever.
  • MethodACSM cycling equation: VO2 = 10.8 × watts ÷ kg + 7, using your best 5 minute power, which is close to power at VO2max. It is an estimate, not a lab test, and assumes a typical efficiency. The trend is more reliable than the absolute number.
